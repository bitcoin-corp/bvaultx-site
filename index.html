How it works

Storage that's already paid for.

We keep your files. You pay a little each month from your own wallet, signed in advance and time-locked so each payment can only go out when its month comes round. Stop, and we stop storing. Nobody holds your money up front.

Live

Lock BSV in your wallet

Open Lock in bWalletX. Lock once, or spread it out day by day, week by week or month by month. The chain keeps each piece until its date.

Coming

It pays month by month

Pick your files and how long to keep them. Your wallet signs one payment per month, each one time-locked to its month. Storage is covered for as long as the lock lasts.

Live

Always yours to take back

Locks are made from your own keys. Lose your phone, and your 12 words still find them and claim them, with or without us.

The idea is simple: pay for storage once, up front, without handing anyone your money. The lock sits in your wallet, pays each month as it comes, and anything left is still yours. The files live on bDriveX; things for sale live on bStoreX.

Items move to Live when you can use them. We tick them off as they ship.
